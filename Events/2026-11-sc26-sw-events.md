# SC26 Software-Related Events

The Supercomputing conference series is one of the premier venues for high-performance computing and computational science and engineering.  Here's a list of events that relate to producing better scientific software and the people who do so.  Please join us there!

- Event Dates: November 15, 2026 - November 20, 2026
- Location: Chicago, Illinois
- Website: https://sc26.supercomputing.org/
- Organizers: The "Software Community"

#### Contributed by [David E. Bernholdt](https://github.com/bernhold)

#### Publication date: October 16, 2026

**Description:** The SC26 program pages, linked from the event title in the table below, provide details including exact time and place, abstracts, etc.  Note that Supercomputing has several different registration categories, and some of the events listed require particular categories of registration.
