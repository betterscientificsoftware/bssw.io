# Guidelines for Respectful Use of AI

AI tools have definitely made it easier to produce more code, documents, and other outputs individually. However, the extra reviewing and validation can end up slowing down the rest of the team.
Therefore, clear guidelines are necessary to ensure that using AI actually adds value instead of creating extra work for colleagues.

The article *Guidelines for Respectful Use of AI* highlights several practical areas that team policies should cover:

- **Review responsibility:** Actually read through, test, and understand whatever the AI generates before sharing it with anyone else.
- **Output quality:** Stop sending unreviewed, half-baked, or messy drafts to your teammates.
- **Code changes:** Try to keep the pull requests small and focused instead of dumping massive chunks of generated code all at once.
- **Documentation:** It's a good idea to keep any written documentation short, clear and actually relevant to what you and your team are doing.
- **Communication:** Be upfront about when you used AI. Also give enough context so others know what they are looking at.
- **Team productivity:** Try to have empathy and think about the time and energy everyone else has to spend reviewing, figuring out, and maintaining the stuff you generate.

Ultimately, productivity should not focus solely on how quickly one person can generate outputs. 
Carefully considered team policies are needed to protect everyone's time, not just speed up individual work.
