# The Better Scientific Software Fellowship at PEARC'26

#### Contributed by [Adam Lavely](https://github.com/adamlavely)

#### Publication date: August 31, 2026

<!-- begin deck -->
The Better Scientific Software Fellowship was engaged throughout PEARC'26!
<!-- end deck -->

One of my favorite conferences of the year is [Practice and Experience in Advanced Research Computing](https://pearc.acm.org) because of the community that is there, the size, and the focus on students. [PEARC'26](https://pearc.acm.org/pearc26/), took place in Minneapolis in July, and was right in line with previous iterations. The conference celebrated it's 10th year, and I marked my 5th in-person attendance, going back to the 2nd iteration in Pittsburgh. I was thankful for the opportunity to help lead the mentorship portion of the the student program this year. 

The [Better Scientific Software (BSSw) Fellowship](https://bssw.io/fellowship) program held a successful In-Conjunction-With event at PEARC during one of the lunch periods. While I did present a dozen or so slides about the fellowship and the application process, the time was mostly alloted to discussion, allowing attendees to discuss and iterate on project ideas. The BSSwF also had its poster out the [Sustainable Horizons Institute](https://shinstitute.org) booth an introduced the fellowship to many conference attendees. 

While the conference had many enjoyable moments, especially interacting with old friends, some BSSw related highlights include:

* The paper [Self-Admitted Technical Debt in Scientific Software: Prioritization, Sentiment, and Propagation Across Artifacts](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3785462.3815801) with co-author [Nasir Eisty](https://bssw.io/fellows/nasir-eisty) (2019 BSSwF Honorable Mention and 2020 BSSw Fellow) taking the best paper of the session award.
* The invited talk [Community Activities to Advance Research Software ](https://pearc.acm.org/pearc26/conference-schedule/#session=a4be9a88-ab5d-4a61-929d-91d377d29cdf) by [Daniel Katz](https://bssw.io/fellows/daniel-s-katz) (2018 BSSw Fellow).
* * The new-this-year Research Software Engineering (People, Practice, and Impact) tract, co-chaired by [Ian Cosden](https://bssw.io/items?author=ian-cosden), and [Pengyin Shan](https://bssw.io/items?author=pengyin-shan), both BSSw blog contributors.
* Listening to [Nitin Sukhija](https://bssw.io/fellows/nitin-sukhija) (2022 BSSw Fellow) talk about his [workshop](https://pearc.acm.org/pearc26/conference-schedule/#session=50e41bf9-7250-4858-818f-7bdca5f0dd21) and [BoF](https://pearc.acm.org/pearc26/conference-schedule/#session=3030e5a3-49cb-4748-b7f4-d25a1b2f9a90).


The BSSw Fellowship community looks forward to continued engagement at the PEARC’27 conference, to be held in the Raleigh, NC.
